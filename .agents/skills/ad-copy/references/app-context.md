# App Context (template — fill me in)

> **This is the only app-specific file in this skill.** Fill in every section below
> for your product before using the skill. Everything else in the skill is
> product-agnostic. Keep the section headings so the skill's instructions still
> resolve.

## 1. Audience

Who the ads target. Include:

- Who they are and what they're trying to do
- What they'd otherwise pay for or resort to (the alternative the product beats)
- Why they're skeptical of this product category
- Where they gather (communities, subreddits)
- The emotional state to write for, and the language they actually use

## 2. Brand voice

The register in two or three sentences (e.g. "a knowledgeable peer, not a
startup"), plus any casing, punctuation, or emoji rules that apply to ad copy.

## 3. Copy rules (the checklist)

The checklist every ad must pass. Include:

- What to lead with, and what never to lead with
- How specific to be (what counts as a concrete identifier for this product)
- What the trust close must communicate
- Who "the shared enemy" is, if the brand has one

## 4. Guardrails (hard-won)

The short list of brand-specific things that must never appear in an ad: claims
to avoid, names that must be used correctly, punctuation bans, positioning rules.

## 5. Product catalog

The authoritative list of what can be advertised:

- Feature/report names with one-line descriptions
- Tiers/pricing and what each includes, if the product has them
- Key phrases from the marketing page worth borrowing
- Real testimonials (verbatim, with attribution) that can be adapted as proof
