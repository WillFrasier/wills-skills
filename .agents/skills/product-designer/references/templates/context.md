# Product design context: <product>

Read by product-designer and copy-writer. Keep it short; link out for depth.

## Product
What it is, in two or three sentences. The core promise.

## Thesis
What the product believes about its users that competitors don't. The bet.

## Audience
Who, in which life situation. Their recurring moments of use. Their competing habits (what they do today instead).

## Roles
For shared products: the roles, how engagement differs between them, and the sensitivities between them.

## Vocabulary
Product terms to use exactly, with one-line definitions.

## Voice
Register, casing, punctuation, emoji policy, words to avoid. (copy-writer treats this section as its app context.)

## Product-specific archetypes
Extra rows for the page-interview archetype table (e.g. a weekly check-in view).

## Research
Links to research, interview syntheses, analytics dashboards, with one line on what each covers.
